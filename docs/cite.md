# Cite us

If the Living Guide has been useful in your work, please cite the transition
paper that introduces it.

## BibTeX

```bibtex
@article{Krause:2026ayh,
  author = "Krause, Claudius and Winterhalder, Ramon and Feickert, Matthew and Nachman, Benjamin",
  title = "{The Living Guide of Machine Learning for Particle Physics}",
  eprint = "2608.09531",
  archivePrefix = "arXiv",
  primaryClass = "hep-ph",
  reportNumber = "MBI-ML-26-05, TIF-UNIMI-2026-10",
  month = "8",
  year = "2026"
}
```

## Citing a specific section

Sections are written as named, timestamped contributions. To cite a specific
section, reference its URL and contributor:

> A. Author, "*Section title*", in *The HEP–ML Living Guide*, accessed YYYY-MM-DD,
> <https://iml-wg.github.io/HEPML-LivingGuide/path/to/section/>

## Citing the archived Living Review

For citations of the predecessor resource, see
[The archived Living Review](archived-review.md). In short: cite
[arXiv:2102.02770](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.02770) for the methodology, and the
[Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/21626667)
(DOI [10.5281/zenodo.21626667](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21626667)) for the
frozen snapshot.
