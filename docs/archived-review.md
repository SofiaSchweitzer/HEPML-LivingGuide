# The archived Living Review

The original [HEP–ML Living Review](https://iml-wg.github.io/HEPML-LivingReview/)
is preserved in its final state as a static archive. It remains a useful
bibliographic record of the HEP–ML literature through 2026.

## What it is

A near-comprehensive list of machine-learning applications in particle physics,
covering 2014–2026. Categories follow the taxonomy of the original Review.

## When to use it

The archived Review is the right resource when you need:

- A historical record of HEP–ML publications through 2026
- A starting point for systematic bibliometric analysis
- Coverage of papers that pre-date the Living Guide

It is **not** the right resource for:

- Orientation in a current subfield — use the relevant section of the Guide instead
- Up-to-date pointers — the Review is frozen
- Curated reading lists — the Review is comprehensive, not selective

- **Web:** <https://iml-wg.github.io/HEPML-LivingReview/>

## How to cite it

The archived Review remains citable. Cite the original arXiv paper for the
methodology and overview, and the Zenodo record (once available) for the
specific archived snapshot.
