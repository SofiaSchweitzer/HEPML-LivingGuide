# The archived Living Review

The original [HEP–ML Living Review](https://iml-wg.github.io/HEPML-LivingReview/)
is preserved in its final state as a static archive. It remains a useful
bibliographic record of the HEP–ML literature until 1 June 2026.

## What it is

A near-comprehensive list of machine-learning applications in particle physics,
covering 2014 until 1 June 2026. Categories follow the taxonomy of the original
Review.

## When to use it

The archived Review is the right resource when you need:

- A historical record of HEP–ML publications until 1 June 2026
- A starting point for systematic bibliometric analysis
- Coverage of papers that pre-date the Living Guide

It is **not** the right resource for:

- Orientation in a current subfield — use the relevant section of the Guide instead
- Up-to-date pointers — the Review is frozen
- Curated reading lists — the Review is comprehensive, not selective

## How to access it

- **Web:** <https://iml-wg.github.io/HEPML-LivingReview/>
- **Repository (read-only):** <https://github.com/iml-wg/HEPML-LivingReview>
- **arXiv:** [arXiv:2102.02770](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.02770)
- **Zenodo archive:** <https://zenodo.org/records/21626667> (DOI [10.5281/zenodo.21626667](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21626667), release `v2026.06.01`)

## How to cite it

The archived Review remains citable. Cite the original arXiv paper for the
methodology and overview, and the [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/21626667)
for the specific archived snapshot — the latter is what pins the literature
coverage to 1 June 2026, so it is the right reference if the exact contents
matter.

```bibtex
@misc{krause_2026_21626667,
  author       = {Krause, Claudius and Winterhalder, Ramon and Feickert, Matthew
                  and Nachman, Benjamin and Raine, John and {HEP-ML Community}},
  title        = {{A Living Review of Machine Learning for Particle Physics}},
  month        = {6},
  year         = {2026},
  publisher    = {Zenodo},
  version      = {v2026.06.01},
  doi          = {10.5281/zenodo.21626667},
  url          = {https://zenodo.org/records/21626667}
}
```
