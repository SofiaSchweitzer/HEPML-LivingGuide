# Reviews & lecture notes

A curated selection of broader resources that span multiple HEP–ML subfields.
For topic-specific reviews, see the relevant [application](../applications/index.md)
or [method](../methods/index.md) section.

## Lecture notes

- **Modern Machine Learning for LHC Physicists**, Plehn, Butter, Dillon, Heimel,
  Krause, Winterhalder ([arXiv:2211.01421](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.01421)) —
  comprehensive lecture notes covering modern deep learning methods as applied
  across LHC physics, suitable as a course textbook.
- **TASI Lectures on Physics for Machine Learning**, Halverson
  ([arXiv:2408.00082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00082)) — TASI lectures on the
  use of physics-inspired ideas in machine learning, including connections to
  formal theory.

## Cross-cutting community resources

- **Les Houches guide to reusable ML models in LHC analyses**, Araz et al.
  ([arXiv:2312.14575](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14575)) — practical guide on
  publishing and reusing trained ML models for collider analyses.
- **Building an AI-native Research Ecosystem for Experimental Particle Physics:
  A Community Vision**, Aarrestad et al.
  ([arXiv:2602.17582](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17582)) — a community vision
  paper outlining the next decade of AI/ML integration in experimental HEP.

## Snowmass reports

- **Snowmass 2021 Computational Frontier CompF03 Topical Group Report: Machine
  Learning**, Shanahan et al.
  ([arXiv:2209.07559](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.07559)) — community summary of
  the state of machine learning in HEP through the Snowmass 2021 process.
- **Machine Learning and LHC Event Generation**, Badger et al.
  ([arXiv:2203.07460](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.07460)) — focused Snowmass
  contribution on ML for event generation.

## Topic-specific reviews

- **The Landscape of Unfolding with Machine Learning**, Huetsch et al.
  ([arXiv:2404.18807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18807)) — a review of ML
  approaches to detector unfolding, with comparisons across methods.
